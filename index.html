A Sanctuary for Unhurried Mornings & Warm Evenings

Designed around natural oak, linen textures, and a sunlit open bakery counter, TASTE & THYME brings together the comfort of a neighborhood cafe and the craft of a modern seasonal kitchen.

Meet the Culinary Lead

Chef Devika Menon & The Hearth Team

“Great cooking respects the ingredient first—giving herbs, grains, and fire enough quiet room to speak.”

Trained across artisan bakeries and seasonal kitchens, Chef Devika leads TASTE & THYME with a focus on fermentation, bright acidity, and comforting textures. Every dish balances familiar warmth with thoughtful culinary detail.
